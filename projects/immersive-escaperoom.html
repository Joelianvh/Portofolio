Immersive Escaperoom

A 3-week escape room built from scratch, with narrative puzzle design, custom electronics and sensor triggers.

My Role Systems programmer & electronics builder (3 weeks)
Tech & tools
Arduino Piezo & distance sensors Servo motors Soldering & electronics Laser cutting Figma & web tech
Links Watch video demo

Context & problem

Built in 3 weeks, this escape room has puzzles that respond to physical actions. That required custom hardware, soldering and sensor integration.

Target audience

Groups of 2 to 6 players who like atmospheric puzzles, and researchers studying physical computing.

Features

Linked puzzle sequences driven by piezo knock sensors, ultrasonic distance sensors, servo-controlled locks and custom HTML/CSS/JS dashboards.

Process

As systems programmer and electronics lead, I soldered custom sensor circuits, laser-cut enclosure props and programmed the Arduinos to read sensor thresholds and move servos when a puzzle was solved.

Takeaways

Building electronics from scratch, from soldering to tuning sensors to laser-cutting enclosures, gave me confidence in hardware and software integration.
