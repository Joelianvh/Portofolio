8 Survivors

“You wake up on a ship while your family sleeps in cryopods. The AI on your wrist deck warns you that the ship is broken and that you need to fix it to stay safe.”

My Role Lead programmer & producer (team of 4, 4 weeks)
Tech & tools
Unity C# PSX aesthetic Game architecture Production & sprint lead
Links Play on Itch.io

Context & problem

Built in 4 weeks by a team of 4, 8 Survivors is a sci-fi thriller built around a cautionary tale. Players move through the tight corridors of a lone starship, do maintenance tasks given by their wrist-deck AI, and find out what is happening on board before it is too late.

Target audience

Fans of retro PSX-era aesthetics, atmospheric sci-fi horror and story-driven games.

Features

Retro PSX-style 3D look, an interactive wrist-deck AI interface, task-based objectives and environmental storytelling.

Process

As lead programmer and producer, I managed our 4-week sprint goals, built modular C# systems in Unity for task tracking and character interaction, and tuned audio and lighting triggers to build tension.

Takeaways

Combining lead programmer and producer duties under a tight deadline taught me to manage scope, iterate quickly and keep the team communicating.
