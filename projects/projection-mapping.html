Projection Mapping

A projection mapping installation that turns physical surfaces into reactive visual canvases, combining spatial storytelling with real-time rendering.

My Role Installation designer & visual technician
Tech & tools
Projection Mapping MadMapper Creative coding Spatial design

Context & problem

Physical space often lacks visual dynamics. The challenge was mapping visuals precisely onto irregular 3D surfaces so the object looks alive.

Target audience

Exhibition visitors, media art fans and event designers looking for immersive storytelling in a space.

Features

Precise surface mapping, real-time layer compositing, and content that reacts to the shape of the object.

Process

Calibrated the projector geometry against the physical objects, made custom visual assets for alignment, and iterated on animation timing to keep the projection in sync with the structure.

Takeaways

Projection mapping taught me that the environment itself is a canvas, and that the strongest visual experiences happen when digital and physical blend together.
