Woodworking

Physical prototypes and interactive enclosures made from wood, combining traditional craft with electronics.

My Role Physical prototype builder & maker
Tech & tools
Woodworking Physical computing Prototyping Fabrication

Context & problem

Many interactive prototypes lack durability and finish. The challenge was building enclosures that work for the electronics and also look good.

Target audience

Makers, interaction designers and fabrication enthusiasts interested in craft and technology.

Features

Custom-cut enclosures for sensors and microcontrollers, hand-finished surfaces and integrated cable management for clean, exhibition-ready builds.

Process

Planned the internal component layout before cutting, used jigs for precise cuts, and sanded and finished the wood to get the feel I wanted.

Takeaways

Working with physical materials grounded my design thinking. The limits of material and space pushed me to make more deliberate decisions than digital prototyping does.
