RocketJump

A fast rocket-jumping action prototype built in Unity during an AGD bootcamp, with movement mechanics, physics design and a full playable build made in one sprint.

My Role Game designer & mechanics developer
Tech & tools
Unity C# Physics & movement Rapid prototyping
Links Play RocketJump on Itch.io

Context & problem

The bootcamp challenge: design and deliver a playable game concept in a short sprint. Built in Unity, RocketJump explores momentum-based movement and rocket knockback physics.

Target audience

Platformer fans, movement tech enthusiasts and game jam players who like fast mechanics.

Features

A core rocket-jumping loop built in Unity, momentum physics, dynamic arena hazards and immediate player feedback.

Process

Focused on movement feel first: tuning velocity impulses, explosion force vectors and gravity scale in C# before building any level layout.

Takeaways

Iterating on movement feel early showed that good game physics can make even a simple level engaging.
