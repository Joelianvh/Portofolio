Ableton Sound Control

A custom physical controller that connects hardware inputs to Ableton Live, so music can be performed with gestures through sensor-mapped MIDI.

My Role Interaction designer & hardware hacker
Tech & tools
Ableton Live MIDI Arduino Physical computing

Context & problem

Standard MIDI controllers limit expressive performance. The goal was to map unusual sensor inputs like pressure, distance and rotation to Ableton parameters, creating new ways to perform.

Target audience

Electronic musicians, live performers and interaction designers interested in alternative music interfaces.

Features

Real-time MIDI mapping of sensor values, custom parameter scaling, low-latency USB serial communication, and a modular design that lets me swap sensors between sessions.

Process

Wired Arduino sensors to read analog inputs, converted the readings to MIDI CC values and sent them over USB to Ableton. Tuned sensitivity curves and deadzones to remove noise and make the controller feel expressive.

Takeaways

Building a custom instrument taught me that interface design is performance design. The physical feel of an input shapes the music it produces.
