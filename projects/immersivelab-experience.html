Immersivelab Experience

A guided lab experience about sensory input, spatial design and interactive technology, spread over several rooms.

My Role Experience designer & installation lead
Tech & tools
Spatial design Sensors Interactive media Installation art

Context & problem

Designing an experience that feels cohesive across several interactive stations, each with its own interaction, while keeping one atmosphere throughout the lab.

Target audience

Students, educators and creative technology researchers visiting the immersive lab.

Features

Several connected interactive stations, responsive ambient lighting, sensor-triggered audio narratives and a visitor flow that builds in intensity from entry to exit.

Process

Mapped the visitor journey before designing the individual stations, so each interaction built on the previous one. Coordinated hardware, software triggers and atmosphere so they worked together.

Takeaways

Curating a multi-station experience taught me to think about the visitor's emotional arc. The best installations guide feeling as much as movement through space.
